Welcome to Your Budget Tracker!

This tool will help you manage your income and track your weekly spending allowance based on real cash flow.

How to Get Started:

  • Click the Settings button in the top right.
  • Go to the Period & Income tab to enter your hourly rate, hours per week, Student Finance total, and PIP rates.
  • Go to the Fixed Outgoings tab to enter your monthly bills (e.g., Apple Care, rent).
  • Once complete, your Base Weekly Allowance will calculate, and the Weekly Spending Tracker will be ready to use!
